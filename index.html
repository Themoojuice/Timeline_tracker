Time on Site

When you got to work and when you left, read from your Google Timeline. The file is read on this device and never uploaded.

1Load your Timeline

How to export it
  1. On your phone: Settings → Location → Location services → Timeline.
  2. Tap Export Timeline data and save the file.
  3. Come back here, choose the file, press Analyse.

Each export holds your whole history, so once a week is plenty. If the menu differs on your phone, try Google Maps → your picture → Your Timeline → ⋮ → Location and privacy settings.
