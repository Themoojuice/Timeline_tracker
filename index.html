Time on Site

When you got to work and when you left, read from your Google Timeline. Your location file is read on this device and never uploaded.

1Load your Timeline

How to export it
  1. On your phone: Settings → Location → Location services → Timeline.
  2. Tap Export Timeline data and save the file.
  3. Come back here and tap Load Timeline export. The newest file is usually at the top of the list.

Each export holds your whole history, so once a week is plenty. This page also keeps what it has seen on this device, so weeks stay here even after Google drops them from your phone.

    Google Sheets link

    not set up

    One-off, about 5 minutes, easiest on a laptop. After that, "Send to Google Sheets" updates the same sheet every time: a tab for the week you're viewing, one with every day, and a weekly summary.

    1. Make a new Google Sheet and name it Time on Site.
    2. In the sheet: Extensions → Apps Script. Delete what's there, paste the script, and save.
    3. Deploy → New deployment. Click the gear, pick Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone. Deploy.
    4. Google will warn that the app isn't verified, because it's your own script. Click Advanced → Go to … (unsafe) → Allow.
    5. Copy the Web app URL, send it to your phone, and paste it here.

    Keep the link private: anyone who has it can write to that sheet, though not read it.
