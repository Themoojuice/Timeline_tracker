Time on Site

How to get the Timeline file
  1. On your phone: Settings → Location → Location services → Timeline.
  2. Tap Export Timeline data and save the file.
  3. Tap Import Timeline here and pick it (newest file is usually at the top).

AVAC forms

One form per week, last 4 weeks. Open a week to check it, change anything, then print.
